Why it needs reasoning

"3 bags 45k each" and "3 bags for 45k" differ by ₦90,000. Market speech is full of shorthand, part payments and speech-to-text errors, and getting the book wrong costs real money. Every entry goes through SERV Reasoning, which works out unit price versus total, credit versus cash, and who the customer is before anything is saved. It reads the record back for confirmation, and asks when something is unclear.
